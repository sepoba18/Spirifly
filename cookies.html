Cookie Policy & Google CMP Consent

Last Updated: October 19, 2026 | Version 1.0

1. What Are Cookies and Web Storage?

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores on your computer or mobile device. It enables the platform to remember your actions and preferences (such as language, active server, and display settings) over time. Spirifly also utilizes Web Storage (localStorage) to preserve your goals and inventory directly within your browser without unnecessary network requests.

2. Types of Cookies and Technologies Used

A. Strictly Necessary and Technical Cookies

These cookies are essential for the safe and reliable operation of the web application. They cannot be disabled in our systems.

B. Local Preference Storage (localStorage)

Stores your local settings so that you don't have to reconfigure the platform on each visit:

  • Selected display language (English, Spanish, etc.).
  • Active game server (America, Europe, Asia) for calculating the official daily reset time (04:00 AM server time).
  • Your tracked characters, weapons, and goals in the planner (in local mode).

C. Google AdSense Advertising Cookies

Google and its advertising partners use cookies (such as __gads, __gpi, IDE) to:

  • Serve relevant ads based on website content or previous visits.
  • Limit the frequency with which you see the same advertisement (frequency capping).
  • Prevent fraud and invalid clicks.

3. Google CMP Consent Management Platform (Consent Mode v2)

We comply with European Union privacy regulations (GDPR), the IAB Europe Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF v2.2), and Google Consent Mode v2. Before serving personalized advertising cookies in regulated jurisdictions, explicit authorization is requested for the following parameters:

Parameter Purpose Initial State
ad_storage Storage of cookies for advertising purposes Denied until user consent
ad_user_data Transmission of user data for advertising purposes to Google Denied until user consent
ad_personalization Personalized advertising and remarketing Denied until user consent
analytics_storage Storage of analytics and measurement cookies Denied until user consent

4. Managing and Revoking Consent

You can change your cookie preferences or revoke consent at any time:

  • In your browser: You can configure your browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari) to block or delete cookies at any time.
  • Ad choice opt-out tools: You can disable ad personalization through Google Ad Settings and Your Online Choices.
